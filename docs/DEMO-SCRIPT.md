# 3-minute demo video: script

Record your screen (Windows: press **Win + Alt + R** with the Xbox Game Bar, or use OBS) with your voice. Open the app first: run `npm run app`, then open http://127.0.0.1:8787 in Chrome at full screen. Speak calmly; the script is about 400 words, which fits in 3 minutes.

---

### 0:00–0:12 · Hook (show: the hero meeting, verdict FAIL at the top)
> "These minutes say all three directors met in Jersey. One of them was in Dubai, and another was still on a plane when the meeting started. Substance Proof caught both, and cited the exact page and the exact second of the recording."

### 0:12–0:35 · The problem (show: the home page)
> "Under Jersey's Economic Substance Law, a company has to show a quorum of its directors was physically in Jersey when the board met, or face penalties of up to a hundred thousand pounds. Singapore has a similar test. Today a company secretary checks this by hand, and the minutes only record what people claimed. I'm an accounting student interning at a trust company, so this is a real job I've seen."

### 0:35–1:20 · Run it live (show: click "Analyse" on m11 in the live list; while it runs, explain)
> "I give it the meeting pack: the minutes, the travel documents, hotel bills and the meeting recording. Muse Spark reads every document, even phone photos and scans, and Muse Voice Transcribe turns the recording into a roll-call. Every fact comes back with the words it was read from."
(When it finishes, scroll to the timeline.)
> "Here's where each director really was. Alice: a hotel in St Helier covers the whole meeting. Bruno: the minutes say St Helier, but his hotel bill and his own words on the recording say Dubai." (Click the recording chip, let it play 3 seconds.) "Chandra: her e-ticket shows the plane landing in Jersey forty minutes after the meeting began."

### 1:20–1:50 · The rules (show: Rule tests table)
> "The verdict isn't decided by the AI. Tested code applies the published law: Article 5(3) of the Jersey law needs a quorum of two physically present. The evidence shows only one, so it fails. Stricter points from guidance are labelled and can be switched off."

### 1:50–2:20 · The agent (show: drafts, click Approve on one, open "Show the agent's tool calls")
> "Then an agent built on Muse Spark's tool calling drafts corrections for the company secretary, quoting both sides. Every tool call is checked by code, and nothing is sent until a person approves."

### 2:20–2:50 · Proof it works (show: README results table on GitHub)
> "I tested it on thirty held-out meetings with phone photos, scans and different formats: every fact, every director and every verdict was right. On a deliberately hostile stress set with blurry photos and handwriting, accuracy drops to about eighty-five percent, but it made zero dangerous errors: when it can't read something, it asks for review instead of guessing."

### 2:50–3:00 · Close (show: the hero verdict again)
> "Substance Proof: the minutes say the board met in Jersey. Now you can prove it. Built with Muse Spark and Muse Voice Transcribe."
