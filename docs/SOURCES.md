# Rule sources (what each rule in the engine is based on)

Information and calculation only. Not legal or tax advice. Fetched 28 Oct 2026.

## Jersey — primary source (verified verbatim)
Taxation (Companies – Economic Substance) (Jersey) Law 2019, version current at fetch (17 Jun 2026).
https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_3_2019

- **Art. 5(3)** — the "directed and managed in Jersey" test (Art. 5(2)(a)) is satisfied if:
  - (a) the board "meets in Jersey at an adequate frequency having regard to the amount of decision-making required at that level";
  - (b) "at such board meetings … there is a quorum of directors physically present in Jersey";
  - (c) "the minutes of such board meetings … record the making of strategic decisions of the company at the meeting";
  - (d) the directors have the necessary knowledge and expertise;
  - (e) "the minutes of all board meetings and the records of the company are kept in Jersey".
- **Art. 5(4)** — the Comptroller may issue guidance on the meaning of "adequate".
- **Art. 9(2)** — penalty for failing the test: maximum **£10,000**. **Art. 9(4)** — failing in the following period: maximum **£100,000**. Art. 9(5): the Comptroller may report to the Minister.

### Jersey — guidance-level rules (SECONDARY sources; verify against Comptroller guidance before relying)
The law itself only says "adequate frequency" and "a quorum". Practitioner summaries of the Comptroller's guidance (Collas Crill, PwC Jersey, Ogier) report these extra points. They are implemented as **toggles marked `guidance`**, never as hard law:
- a **majority of board meetings** should be held in Jersey (not all);
- a quorum, not all directors, must be physically present at an ordinary meeting;
- for meetings deciding strategic matters / core income-generating activities, a **majority of directors** should be physically present in Jersey;
- a **sole director** must be physically present in Jersey when deciding;
- for a **corporate director**, look through to the individuals who act for it;
- isolated decisions outside Jersey may be tolerated only if the Jersey decisions clearly outweigh them.
- Ogier checklist (1 Sep 2025, https://www.ogier.com/news-and-insights/guides-and-factsheets/substance-checklist-for-directors-of-jersey-companies/): quorum "physically present in Jersey", meetings "at adequate frequency", minutes reflect strategic decision-making. No numeric frequency given.

## Singapore — primary source (verified verbatim)
IRAS, "Tax Residency of a Company / Certificate of Residence", control and management.
https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/corporate-income-tax/basics-of-corporate-income-tax/tax-residency-of-a-company

- Control and management = "making of decisions on strategic matters"; usually determined by the location of board meetings where strategic decisions are made. It is "a question of fact"; IRAS considers all facts.
- **Virtual meeting technology:** a board meeting involving it "will generally be regarded as having strategic decisions made in Singapore if either of the following conditions is met":
  1. "At least 50% of the directors (with the authority to make strategic decisions) are physically in Singapore during the meetings"; or
  2. "Chairman of the Board of Directors (if the company has such an appointment) is physically in Singapore during the meeting."
- Other factors IRAS considers (indicators, not pass/fail): whether directors are based in or outside Singapore; whether the local director makes strategic decisions; whether key employees are in Singapore; place of incorporation is "not necessarily indicative".
- Applies to residency for a Year of Assessment based on the preceding calendar year.
- EY Singapore tax alert, Feb 2024 (secondary): describes the same two conditions.
